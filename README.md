# Discussion Discover

**Live Randomizer Website:** https://keanunm.github.io/discussion-discover-randomizer/

**Players:** 4-6 players  
**Roles:** 1 Judge, 1 Impostor, Remaining players are Good Guys

## Setup

1. Choose one player to be the **Judge**.
2. Deal everyone a role card (or use the **Role Card Dealer** on the [Discussion Discover Randomizer website](https://keanunm.github.io/discussion-discover-randomizer/)). Ask all players to look at their card secretly before placing it face down.
3. Ask everyone to hide their cards and put their heads down (close their eyes).
4. The Judge asks the **Impostor** to wake up and silently signal who they are.
5. Ask the Impostor to hide their card and put their head back down.

## Discovering the Card

1. Shuffle the big blue stack of object cards, or open the [**Discussion Discover Randomizer** (https://keanunm.github.io/discussion-discover-randomizer/)](https://keanunm.github.io/discussion-discover-randomizer/) and click **Randomize Object!** to draw a secret object.
2. The Judge taps each player one by one (except the Impostor) to wake up and see what the object card is. Once a player has seen it, they put their head back down.
3. **Important:** Do **not** show the card to the Impostor (if using the website, click **Hide Card** before waking everyone up).

## Discussion Phase

1. The Judge asks everyone to wake up.
2. Roll a die (or click **Roll Die** on the [Discussion Discover Randomizer website](https://keanunm.github.io/discussion-discover-randomizer/)) to pick a random starting player. Going clockwise one by one, each player gives a single description of what the object is like.
   - Your description should not be too obvious (or the Impostor will figure out the object), nor too vague (or the other players will think you are the Impostor).

## Voting Phase

1. Once everyone has given one description, players discuss and point out who said the weirdest or most irrelevant description.
2. Everyone votes on who to eliminate. **Skipping a vote is not allowed.**
3. **Ties:** If two or more players receive an equal majority of votes, resolve it with a tiebreaker (such as Rock-Paper-Scissors) or skip the elimination and redo the round (return to the **Discussion Phase**).
4. **Elimination:** If a player receives a clear majority of votes (no ties), they are eliminated. They must reveal their role card and cannot speak during future meetings.

## Winning and Losing

- **Good Guys Win:** If the majority votes out the Impostor, the Good Guys immediately win.
- **Redo the Round:** If the majority votes out a Good Guy, the game continues—return to the **Discussion Phase** for another round.
- **Impostor Wins (Good Guys Lose):** If only 1 Impostor and 1 Good Guy remain, the Good Guys lose automatically (since the Impostor can no longer be outvoted).
